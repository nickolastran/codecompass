# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 12, 2026_

This policy explains what CodeCompass ("we", "us") collects when you use the hosted service, and what we do with it. If you self-host CodeCompass, your instance's operator controls your data, not us.

## What we collect

- **Account data.** Your email address, plus the basic profile your sign-in provider (GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket) shares when you sign in with OAuth or an email code.
- **Repository access.** Installation IDs and access tokens for the Git provider apps you connect, and metadata about the repositories you choose to index.
- **Repository contents.** Source code from repositories you index. We clone it so we can generate documentation and build search indexes (embeddings and a knowledge graph).
- **Generated content.** The documentation pages, code summaries, and indexes built from your code.
- **Chat history.** The questions you ask and the answers CodeCompass gives.
- **Organization data.** The organizations you create or join, their members, and invitations.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use your code to train models.

## How we use it

We use this data only to run CodeCompass: signing you in, indexing your repositories, generating documentation, answering your questions about your code, and managing access within your organizations.

## Third parties

- **Supabase** hosts our database and handles authentication.
- **Your Git provider** (GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket) supplies sign-in and repository access.
- **LLM providers.** Indexing may send code excerpts to an OpenAI-compatible model API to generate summaries and documentation. Chat answers are generated by a model we run on our own infrastructure.

## Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account or organization is active. Removing a repository deletes its indexes and generated documentation. To delete your account and its data, open an issue or contact us at the link below. Uninstalling our app from your Git provider revokes our access to your repositories.

## Security

Access to repositories and documentation is limited to members of the owning organization. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
